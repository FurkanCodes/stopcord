# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The first user is the person building it: an engineer who runs coding agents across about ten projects at once. They check the app dozens of times a day, between tasks, often with terminals open beside it. Other agentic engineers may use it later, but they don't shape the first version.

## Product Purpose

Sawtooth (working title; earlier "Little Factories") puts each project in a factory: its terminals, its knowledge base, its rules, its triggers and its tickets, kept together. The home screen shows every factory at once, so the engineer can see what needs them without opening a pile of terminals. Success means a whole week goes by without opening a terminal outside the app to check on a project.

## Positioning

One glance shows what every project's agents are doing and what needs you, and any part of any factory can be changed from where you see it.

## Brand Personality

Calm, exact, industrial. It reads like a well-run factory floor: quiet until something needs a person, then it says so plainly. The voice is short and literal, and it names things by what they are.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS dashboards with big number tiles. Node editors full of tangled wires that carry no meaning. Anything that makes a terminal feel like a toy.

## Design Principles

- Show what needs a person before anything else.
- Every part shows its real state, and every part can be changed where it's shown.
- The canvas and the list show the same factory, so switching between them loses nothing.
- Work with any harness and any model. No screen assumes one.
- Quiet by default. Color and motion only mark state.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Status is never shown by color alone: a working lamp is filled, an idle lamp is a ring, and anything that needs you also says so in a word. Every lamp has a text label for screen readers, but quiet states show no words on screen. Keyboard reachable throughout, and motion respects reduced-motion settings.
